Common questions

Does Jern need an account?
No. Everything is stored locally on your device.

What are the Gemini and Oura settings for?
Both are optional. Add your own Gemini API key in Settings to enable the Coach tab's AI tips. Connect your own Oura account to feed recovery data into those tips. Neither is required for the app to work.

Where's my data stored?
On your device only, via Apple's SwiftData framework. See the Privacy Policy for details.
