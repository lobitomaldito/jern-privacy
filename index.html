Jern — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-24

Jern is a workout tracking app. This page explains what data it handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

Your workouts, exercises, personal records, body metrics, and exercise photos are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is not sent to us — we do not operate any servers and have no access to it.

Optional third-party integrations

Jern includes two optional integrations, each disabled until you add your own API key in Settings:

  • Gemini (Google) — if you add a Gemini API key, your training data is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API to generate coaching tips. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Oura — if you connect an Oura account, your recovery and readiness data is fetched directly from Oura's API to your device. See Oura's Privacy Policy.

API keys and tokens for these integrations are stored in your device's Keychain. Neither integration is required for Jern to work.

Camera

Jern can use your camera to attach a photo to an exercise. Photos are stored locally with your other workout data and are never uploaded anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No account creation or sign-in required
  • No selling or sharing of your data
